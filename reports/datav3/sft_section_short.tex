\subsection{Validation under Model Adaptation}
\label{sec:sft_validation}

We further validate the proposed routing design when paired with LoRA fine-tuning of
Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct on $162 \times 6 = 962$ Q1-Q6 training examples (LoRA rank 32,
$\alpha = 64$, 5 epochs, bf16, single A800 80GB). The trained adapter is evaluated on the
held-out Q7 set under the same prompt configurations as Table~\ref{tab:ablation_results}.
Table~\ref{tab:sft_results} reports the two SFT configurations that yield statistically
significant gains over the BASE-model counterpart (95\% bootstrap CIs disjoint, marked
$\star$): (i) \textbf{SFT B4-NoRule} reduces macro-TED by $28\%$ ($2.516 \rightarrow 1.821$)
and lifts criterion uniqueness by $30\%$ ($0.455 \rightarrow 0.592$); (ii) \textbf{SFT
B4-PlusPeer} achieves a $7.4\times$ improvement in non-V recall ($0.025 \rightarrow 0.185$).
These gains demonstrate that the structured CAPS routing remains effective after model
adaptation, with task-specific prompt simplifications further amplifying the trained signal.
